# Bank logo assets

Bank logos identify their respective institutions; trademarks remain the property of those institutions.

- White SVG logos and brand colors: https://github.com/omise/banks-logo (MIT; see LICENSE-omise.txt).
- BOC, ANZ and OCBC: https://github.com/auraveni/global-bank-logos (MIT; see LICENSE-global-bank-logos.txt).
- Additional logos from Wikimedia Commons, bank-sourced artwork: File:EXIM Bank Logo.svg, File:SME Development Bank Logo.svg, File:Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Holdings Logo.svg, File:Indian Overseas Bank Logo.svg, File:RHB Logo.svg, File:TTB Logo.svg. See the corresponding file pages for author/source/PD-textlogo notices.
- Institution reference: https://www.bot.or.th/th/fi-branch.html
- Domestic routing codes cross-checked against https://devzone.2c2p.com/docs/bank-code and BOT institution code references. The catalog includes Thai banks, specialized government banks and foreign banks operating in Thailand; obsolete standalone TMB/Thanachart/RBS entries are omitted.

All image files are served locally. No logo network request is required at runtime.
